ABOUT ME

Who I Am

I am Yeasir Arafat, a Multimedia Journalist, Reporter and Entrepreneur with a passion for storytelling, digital media and creative communication.

I am currently working as a Multimedia Journalist at The Daily Jatiyo Orthoniti News Portal, where my work focuses on news reporting, digital content production, video journalism, scriptwriting, voice-over and creating engaging news content for online platforms.

Alongside my journalism career, I am also an entrepreneur. I run Amie's Mart, a business focused on buying and selling pre-owned cars and motorcycles.
