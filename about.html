01 — ABOUT ME

More Than A Profession

I am Yeasir Arafat, a journalist and multimedia professional currently working as a Multimedia Journalist at The Daily Jatiyo Orthoniti News Portal.

My work focuses on news reporting, digital content production, video journalism, scriptwriting, voice-over and creating engaging news content for online platforms.

I am passionate about reporting real stories, exploring issues that matter to people and presenting information through responsible, creative and meaningful digital storytelling.

05 — TRAVEL & EXPLORATION

The Road Is Part of My Story

I love traveling and exploring new places. Traveling has always been an important part of my life because it allows me to experience new people, cultures, environments and stories.

I have personally traveled across almost 60 districts of Bangladesh, often driving my own motorcycle or car.

These journeys have given me the opportunity to experience different communities and ways of life from a much closer perspective.

For me, travel is more than visiting a destination. It is about discovering people, understanding places and collecting experiences that shape the way I see the world.
